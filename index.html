A LITTLE ABOUT US

Engineering expertise,
human understanding.

ARCOVA is a Dhaka-based, engineer-led firm delivering integrated solutions for residential, commercial, institutional and public-sector projects. We bring design, structural thinking, estimation, construction and specialist support into one coordinated process.

Our approach is simple: understand the brief, respect the site, communicate clearly and make decisions that work in the real world.

WHAT WE DO

Everything connected.
Nothing overlooked.

From early-stage planning to on-site delivery, our service areas work together to keep your project moving in the right direction.

01 / DESIGN⌂

Architectural planning & design

Concept development, 2D layouts, working drawings, 3D views and approval-plan support.

02 / ENGINEERING⌁

Structural analysis & design

Structural modeling, analysis, member design and construction drawings.

03 / COST▤

Estimation & BOQ

Cost estimation, bills of quantities, project profiles and scheduling support.

Civil construction & supervision

Project profiles & scheduling
